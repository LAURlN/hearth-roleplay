# Roleplay

A shared fictional story in which characters have separate perspectives and beliefs.

## Language

**Character**:
A person in the story with their own personality, intentions, choices, and knowledge.
_Avoid_: Unit

**Narrator**:
The storyteller who knows the world's secrets and manages surroundings and consequences. Each character retains control of their own speech, intentions, and choices.
_Avoid_: Character controller

**Illustrator**:
The observer who decides which established moments deserve an illustration and how to depict them.

**Presence**:
A character's physical whereabouts at a particular point in the story, including their arrival at and departure from a location.
_Avoid_: Speaking status

**Character knowledge**:
What a character has learned or believes, which may differ from what actually happened.
_Avoid_: World truth

**World event**:
Something that happens in the story, whether or not any particular character learns about it.

**Public information**:
Information available to an audience through shared experience, news, online posts, or other public channels. Its availability does not mean every character has encountered or believes it.
_Avoid_: Universal knowledge

**Exposure**:
A character's encounter with information through perception, communication, or a public source.

**Out-of-character instruction (OOC)**:
A player's instruction to the narrator outside the fiction. Characters cannot perceive it.

**Private thought**:
An internal experience of a character that other characters cannot directly perceive.

**Story time**:
Time within the fiction, including elapsed time between interactions and explicit time skips.

**Rewind**:
A return to an earlier point in the conversation and the corresponding state of the story, including everyone's knowledge.

**Story branch**:
A continuation of the story from a shared earlier point. Events and knowledge belonging only to another continuation are not part of this branch.

**Illustration history**:
The continuing record of illustration instructions and resulting images for a story branch, used to preserve visual continuity.
